# Project Plan — Phone as Key

## Project sentence
Phone as a key, to access a private survey, for participants.

## The relation
A randomly generated sequence of physical actions unlocks access to the survey.

## Actions
- Shake the phone
- Swipe
- Press and hold

## Where
Public places — the school hall, the school gate, the cafeteria.

## What I am testing
Whether the approach works: a sequence of physical actions, done in public on the
participant's own phone, can unlock access to the survey.
