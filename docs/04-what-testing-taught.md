# What the testing session taught you (final)

1. The tester felt three actions ended too soon to feel like unlocking anything. This surprised me — I had assumed that fewer steps would be better, but in practice too few steps made the unlock feel unearned.

2. The instructions were hard to read. The action name was large, but the part that decided success — the direction, or how long to hold — was small and sat underneath it, so the tester missed it.

3. The motion sensor was asked for partway through the run rather than once at the start. The tester did not notice the request and had to be told where it was before continuing.
